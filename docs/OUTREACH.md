**Route/type:** [Developer Portal](https://developer.crazygames.com/) and the [official FAQ/contact route](https://docs.crazygames.com/faq/); advertising revenue share is described for eligible full launches. Basic Launch has no monetization and can omit the SDK; Full Launch requires the SDK, per the [requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/intro/).

**Confirmed restrictions:** [gameplay requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/gameplay/) require PEGI 12 compliance and limit cross-promotion/external ads. [PEGI's own guidance](https://pegi.info/what-do-the-labels-mean) distinguishes gambling themes from gambling simulation, the latter falling under PEGI 18. **Inference:** a slot simulation poses a serious eligibility mismatch even without real money; do not assume acceptance or claim this prototype has a rating.

**Location/payment:** the FAQ welcomes submissions worldwide. The [payout guide](https://docs.crazygames.com/payouts/) uses Tipalti and country-dependent methods. That is not confirmation of this owner's India payout eligibility. Confirm content classification, onboarding/payment availability, revenue basis, exclusivity and public MIT/portfolio rights before doing SDK work.

**Tailored inquiry opener:** “Your PEGI 12 requirement makes me unsure whether this demo-credit-only slot instrument is eligible at all. Could you confirm that first, before I submit or integrate an SDK?”
